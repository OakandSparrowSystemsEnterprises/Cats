# Octa, the eight-sided planet (The Dice System)

Octa is the third world of Ruby's Dice System, the d8, added on 2026-10-17 (Ruby: "cool now lets add octa (or octo ...)"). It ships inside Tetra's page, like Hexa: `pipeline/world.html` is a fragment (scoped CSS under `#oapp`, ids starting with `o`, the hook `window.octa`) that `../tetra/pipeline/build.py` inserts at `{{OCTA}}`. It has no map pipeline yet: nothing on it is discovered or drawn (Ruby: "so the first side hasent been discoverd or drawn yet"), so the fragment draws its eight blank sides itself, on a canvas, each with its number on the die. Tetra's rules apply (read `../tetra/CLAUDE.md`): never invent canon, no em dashes anywhere, test before shipping, Ruby's spellings exactly inside quotes. Read `HANDOFF.md` for the ledger and the open questions.

## What exists (draft 2, 2026-10-17)

- The names: Ruby's rule of 2026-10-17 for every planet, "witchever is the hedron name minus \"hedron\"": Octa, Icosa (Icoso before), Icositetra (Icosotetra before). Before the rule, Octa by the vote Ruby called for ("me, daddy, and you will vote (daddy votes octa, i vote octo)"): Ruby Octo, Daddy Octa, Claude Octa (the pattern of her Cuba renaming, since made her rule, and the family sound of Tetra, Hexa, Octa, Deca, Dodeca). Two to one. She can overrule it.
- The sides: eight, named by their number on the die for now (Ruby: "the sides are just named by the number on the dice"), until she picks two more directions to join Hexa's six or keeps the numbers. The World tab lists five candidate pairs she asked for ("give me at least 3 examples"): Dawn and Dusk, Zenith and Nadir, Windward and Leeward, Over and Under, Sunward and Starward, plus the corner-naming idea as a reading. None is chosen.
- The die: a regular octahedron (`R` = 1.45, corners on the axes). `OCTANTS` gives each number its octant: 1, 3, 5, 7 round the top corner (1 facing the viewer at the start, then round to the viewer's left), 2, 4, 6, 8 their opposites, so opposite sides add up to 9 as on a d8. Which number sits where is a reading. Each side's picture (`sidePicture`) is slate with a dashed edge, its number and "not yet discovered"; `TEX_SCALE` maps the side's own plane onto a 512 px canvas.
- Since her second message of the day ("give octa the same star, a d7 moon, a turn, a calender with one more month than hexa and a restart day that is one and three fourths of a day pleese"): Tetra's star (`SUN0`, the glow in `drawStars`, the sides lit by it, dimmed by an eclipse); the die turns once a day leftward (`spinAngle`, `cam.model`, `worldNormal`; the camera can follow a side as on Hexa); the d7 moon (`moonMesh`: a five-sided prism, `MOON_PRISM` = 0.75 by the solid-angle rule, faces 0 to 4 oblong with one to five craters, 5 and 6 the pentagon ends with six and seven, `FS2`; `moonState` and `moonModel` as Hexa's: opposite the star, the swing on the 14th, rolling once a day); the calendar (`WORLD.calendar`: 15 months of 28 days, the fifteenth unnamed after Amberary, the Restart Day 1.75 days after December, `YEAR` 421.75, `dateParts`, Tetra's day names); phases on the side cards (`phaseOf`, `sideLight`). Readings everywhere Ruby gave only the headline: the moon's shape and ways, the turn's direction and the day's length, the unnamed month's place, the week's names. Unwritten and not drawn: size, fields, seasons, the year count, Octa's place among the worlds.
- The dock shows the date ("Starday · 1 January"; "Restart Day · 1¾ days"; "Starday · day 3 of the unnamed month") and the day of the year of 421¾ with no year or season, the Spin and Day sliders, Play/Pause, −1 and +1 year, Reset, the eight side buttons and the Codex button; the Spin slider and Play/Pause act on the shared clock (`dice.time`, through getters on `state` as in Hexa).
- Codex: World (the world, the vote, the directions question, the Dice System list, Still unwritten), Sides (the eight, each "Not discovered or drawn yet", side 1 in Ruby's words), Side (the chosen side's card with its opposite).
- Hook `window.octa`: `state`, `facing()`, `sides()`, `opposite(id)`, `face(id, fly)`, `setYaw(yaw, pitch)`, `setSpin(on)`, `syncTime()`, `setDay(d)`, `date()`, `months()`, `yearDays()`, `moonDie()`, `moon()`, `phase(id)`, `starScreen()`, `sunDir()`, `release()`, `centre(id)` (screen point of a side's centre), `sample(id)` and `sampleAt(fx, fy)` (pixels). `../tetra/tests/octa.js` uses them.
- Bridge: `dice.ready('octa', start)` and `dice.snapshot('octa', ...)` (baseYaw, pitch, dist, selectedSide); `dice.show('octa')` toggles `away` on `#oapp`; `dice.world` reads `octa` when it is shown.
