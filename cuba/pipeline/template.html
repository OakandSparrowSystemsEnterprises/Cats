The world

CUBAThe cube planet · The Dice System

A world with six square sides, the second world of Ruby’s Dice System after Tetra.

Sides
Six, named like Tetra’s with two more: Canon, East, West, Far, North and South. North is the top and South the bottom. Looking at Canon, East is on its left, West on its right and Far behind it.
Drawn so far
The Canon side: a central island whose four arms reach the sides and connect onto the East, West, North and South sides, with a small hole in the middle that takes you through the world to the Far side. The other five sides are not yet charted beyond that.
Place
Farther from the star than Tetra. Ruby: “if there were 5 magical fields, and tetra was in the first one, cuba would be in the last one (going away from the star would be the direction they were stacked in)”.

The island and the hole

From Ruby’s picture of the Canon side (2026-10-04): a broad island shaped like a cross, with an arm toward the middle of each side and rounded bends at the corners. In the middle she wrote “to far side”: the small hole that takes you to the opposite side of the world. Ruby: “the arms connect to the north south east and west faces”, so the land crosses those four edges; how far it goes on each of those sides is unwritten, and it is drawn stopping a short way in. The island’s shape is drawn from the description of her picture. Neither the island nor the hole has a name yet.

How Cuba was reached

“lets imagine that amber the explorer makes a chamber out of a tree and (calculated corectly) launches of ither spikia or the far side using majic, gets flung by the moving moon (going in or out of solar eclipse) gets flung by the star and lands on the cannon face of cuba (year 1650)”

Ruby, 2026-10-04, in her words. Amber the explorer, in a chamber made from a tree, launched by magic from Spikia or from Tetra’s Far side, flung on by the moving moon at an eclipse and then by the star, and landed on the Canon side of Cuba in the year 1650. Which launch place it was is not decided.
