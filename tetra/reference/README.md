# reference/

Not needed for the build. What is here:

- `ruby/comics/`: Ruby's comics of the key events (2026-10-03), two pages, years -2000 to 0. Transcribed in `HANDOFF.md`.
- `ruby/`: Ruby's sketches. Sapphire's West chart (and an enhanced copy), the central island and Tropicia, the central island nations, Amethyst's South sketch and the continent nations, Obsidian's Far sketch and the far nations, the notebook page with the deltas (Wetia), the Umbrella Tree Zinnia sketch and its cutout.
- `art-versions/`: the four versions of the Canon painting; v4 (28-day) is `pipeline/art_latest.png`.
- `tetra_world_spin_original.html`, `original_spin_page_phone.png`: Joshua's original spinning tetrahedron page, before any of this.
- `screenshots-v10/`: what version 10 looks like on desktop and phone, plus the six-seam sheet (composed by hand from `tests/out/seam_*.png`; `npm run seams` writes the six files, not the sheet).
- `screenshots-v11/`: version 11 (2026-10-03): home, the Canon face card and its chart, a Whiteland card, the calendar with the years, the moon, the key events, the Far, West and South faces, the Mount Celestial card, the World tab, two phone views, the phone tap, the moon and the eclipse on desktop and phone (`moon_*`, `eclipse_*`), the moon mid-swing (`moon_swing`), Mount Celestial's peak (`peak_far`), the year buttons (`years_*`), and the six-seam sheet. Later the same day: `eclipse_card.png` and `eclipse_regions.png` (the Eclipse reading), `unwritten.png`, `phone_calendar.png`. From 2026-10-04: `star_level.png` (the first morning, Starday 1 January, the star level), `calendar.png`, `calendar_strip.png` and `restart_day.png` (the strip in Ruby's order from March, the Restart Day closing the year), `week.png` (the day names), `eclipse_all.png` (every place reading Eclipse), `celestial_eclipse.png`, `celestial.png`, `phone_hud.png`, `phone_strip.png`. Cuba, in the same page since 2026-10-04: `cuba_home.png` (the Canon side, the Plus Continent and the Canyon, the star at the edge), `cuba_world_tab.png`, `cuba_canyon_card.png`, `cuba_eclipse.png` (the 14th of January, the d5 moon in front of the star), `cuba_phone.png`, `cuba_phone_card.png`. All retaken that evening after Tetra's mechanics came to Cuba, and again that night with the painted land, the Regions tab (`cuba_regions.png`), the Canyon card with its Through the Canyon button (`cuba_canyon_card.png`), Cuba in Tetra's sky in the rain season (`tetra_cuba_sky.png`) and Tetra in Cuba's sky (`cuba_tetra_sky.png`). Late that night `cuba_home.png`, `cuba_world_tab.png` and `cuba_canyon_card.png` were retaken with the fair d5 (the five-sided rod with pointed ends over Cuba, the Montezuma under Life of Cuba, Ammolite's map on the Canyon card), and `tetra_moon_d3.png` is new: Tetra's three-sided rod with its pointed ends in the home view. (`moon_desk.png` and the other `moon_*` files are still the 2026-10-03 pictures.)
- `previews/`: marker previews of the three generated maps, the maps sheet, the edge check, the blocky-edge check, the outline preview.

Provenance: the handoff arrived as three archives, `tetra-repo-v10.zip`, `tetra-reference-ruby-and-art.zip` and `tetra-reference-screenshots.zip`, each with a top-level `tetra/` folder. The two reference archives were split off to stay under the chat's upload limit; both are unpacked here in full, so nothing further needs unzipping.
