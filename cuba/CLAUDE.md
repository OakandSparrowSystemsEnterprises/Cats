# Hexa, the cube planet (The Dice System)

Hexa is Ruby's cube planet, the second world of The Dice System after Tetra. It was called Cuba from 2026-10-04 until 2026-10-10 (Ruby: "the hedron name for a cube was a hexahedron So Cuba should be called hexa"); the folder, the test file, the hook `window.cuba`, the ids beginning with c and the `{{CUBA_*}}` placeholders keep the old name, every shown name is Hexa. Read `../tetra/CLAUDE.md` first: its ground rules are the rules here too (canon comes from Ruby by chat, relayed by Joshua; never invent canon, mark every reading as a reading; no em dashes anywhere, `make audit` fails on them; test before shipping; Ruby's spellings exactly). Then read `HANDOFF.md` here (state, the canon ledger, the open questions). Joshua is the client; Ruby is the author.

## What exists (draft 8, 2026-10-10)

Hexa is a second world inside Tetra's page (Ruby: "it neends to be in the same file as tetra"): `../tetra/pipeline/template.html` carries a Tetra/Hexa switch under the title (`#diceWorlds`, remembered in `localStorage` as `dice.world`) and inserts Hexa's fragment at `{{CUBA}}`. The world not shown sleeps (class `away`: hidden, its frame loop idle). Hexa shares Tetra's dark purple star (the same direction `SUN0`, drawn a little smaller: it is farther out).

A WebGL cube with six square sides, turning leftward once a day like Tetra, with Tetra's star and two field rings (gold magical, blue magnetic, a little stronger than Tetra's). The Canon side is drawn: the Plus Continent (Ruby's name for her central island, a broad cross with rounded bends at the corners) whose four arms reach the sides and connect onto the East, West, North and South sides (her words), with the Canyon in its middle, the small hole that takes you through the world to the Far side. Since 2026-10-06 the East, West, North and South sides are drawn from Ruby's sketches of the explorers' maps (`design_side`, `render_side`, `ARM_REACH` in `mapgen.py`; Aquamarine's North, Axinite's East, Benitoite's South, Chrysoberyl's West), with the arms reaching in as far as the sketches show, every land shape a reading of her strokes and nothing named; since 2026-10-10 the two ring features are portals in open sea (`PORTALS` in `mapgen.py`, purple swirls painted on the water, a reading) and the East crescent is gone. Since 2026-10-05 the Far side is drawn from her words: a round main island of a quarter of the face about the Canyon's other end (`FAR_R`), land on the north-east and south-east corners (`FAR_CORNER_A/B`, quarter ellipses, an eighth each: the wedge shape and the equal split are readings), the face half water (asserted), and north of the hole the volcano: a dark cone mesh in the page (`volcano`, `VOLCANO_R`, `VOLCANO_H`, drawn with `prog2` and its `uTint`/`uGlow` uniforms) whose crater glows and throws sparks (`drawLava`, on the `cfx` overlay) through every Restart Day (`erupting()`); cards for the island, the volcano (Erupting now or Quiet) and the corner lands, none of them named. A Codex panel holds the World tab (her words and the readings), the six sides and a card per side or place. Tetra's mechanics carried over (Ruby: "transfer core mecanics from tetra to cuba"): `state.day` with the spin and day sliders, Pause and Reset, the HUD date on Hexa's calendar (14 months of 28 days, Amberary the fourteenth between Tredesember and March, the Restart Day of 1.5 days closing the 393.5-day year; `dateParts`, `seasonFor` with Amberary's two halves, `rainAmount`, the strip `renderYear` from March, no year number), the d5 moon (`moonMesh`, `moonState`, `moonModel`, `prog2`: a prism along the orbit axis, one to five craters a side, phases over the month, the eased swing and eclipse on the 14th), the phase words (`placeLight`, `phaseOf`, `phaseFor`) on cards and in the Sides list, Follow this side and Release view (`setFollow`, `camYaw`), rain over the Canon side (`drawRain`), the field rings (`drawFields`). The star is drawn only where it is (`lastStar`, no edge clamp: Ruby, "the sun moves because it refuses to go of the frame, remove this"). Since the night of 2026-10-04: one clock for both worlds (`window.dice.time`, bound into `state.day`, `state.playing`, `state.speed`; `dice:show` resyncs the controls), Amberary between January and February, the Restart Day 1.5 days (the year 393.5 days), year 50 of Hexa (`yearNow`), Tetra's day names (`weekdayOf`), year buttons, the d5 as a fair five-sided long die (`moonMesh`, `MOON_SIDES`: a pentagonal rod with pointed ends, the rod a reading among fair five-sided dice; since the late night the pyramid is gone, Ruby: "a fair d5 for cuba"), the rings hidden only behind the cube's own outline (`cubeQuads`, `inQuad`), rain on every side turned toward the viewer (`drawRain`), Tetra in Hexa's sky (`drawOtherWorld`, `OTHER_DIR`, a reading), a Regions tab (places grouped under their sides, `renderSides`), the explorers, the plants (everything that grows on Tetra, Ruby) and the Montezuma tree on the World tab, and the dock's date column fixed at 272 px with the day number blank (so nothing moves as the days slide).

The Plus Continent is drawn from a written description of Ruby's photo, not from the photo itself (it had not reached the repository): Ruby's original photographed drawing, visually transcribed by ChatGPT, with the arm connections subsequently confirmed by Ruby. The shape is to be checked against the photo when it arrives (`reference/ruby/README.md`, which also lists her four sketches of the other sides). ## Geometry conventions

- Cube of half-size 1 at the origin, spinning about the North-South axis (Y). `FACES` in the template: `canon` +Z, `far` -Z, `west` +X, `east` -X, `north` +Y (top), `south` -Y (bottom). Each face has a `right` and an `up` vector; texture row 0 is the top of the face, column 0 its left.
- Ruby's arrangement (her net: East, Canon, West, Far in a row, North above Canon, South below): looking at Canon with North up, East is on the LEFT and West on the RIGHT, Far behind. So `east` is -X and `west` is +X. Keep it; it is hers. North's `up` points toward Far, South's `up` toward Canon, as the net unfolds.
- Face textures are 1024 x 1024 px. Each side has its own texture; the Plus Continent's arm enters it from the edge shared with Canon (East: right edge, West: left, North: bottom, South: top) at Canon's arm width, so the edges agree. Reading her sketches: the letters at a sketch's sides name the neighbours; North has Far at the top and East on the left, East has Far on the left and Canon on the right, West has Canon on the left and Far on the right, South has Canon at the top and East on the left (her South sketch was drawn turned a quarter). `anchors.json` holds marker positions in face px per side. `facePoint(side, px, py)` in the template maps them onto the cube.
- Hexa turns leftward once a day like Tetra (`state.spin -= dt*speed*TAU/DAY_SECONDS`, `DAY_SECONDS` 30 as in Tetra; Ruby 2026-10-04: "same as tetra").

## world.html, where things are

`WORLD` (sides, places, lore: her words verbatim), `FACES`, `SUN0` and `STAR_SIZE` (Tetra's star direction, the glow drawn in `drawStars` as on Tetra, at the edge toward the star when it is out of view), `ringPoints`/`strokeRing`/`drawFields` (the two fields on the `cfx` overlay canvas, occluded by the world taken as a ball of radius 1.62), the shaders `VS`/`FS` (lit from `SUN0`), `buildMarkers`/`updateMarkers`, the Codex (`showTab`, `openCodex`, `renderSides`, `renderPlace`, `selectSide`, `selectPlace`), the dock (six face buttons from `WORLD.sides`, Codex, Pause/Spin, Reset), pointer handling (drag turns, wheel zooms, a tap on the world selects the facing side), the frame loop, and the debug hook `window.cuba = {state, face(id, fly), facing(), neighbours(), markers(), sample(id), sampleAt(fx, fy), starPixel(), setSpin(on), select(id)}` used by the tests (`sample` reads the drawn pixel under a marker, `starPixel` the sky pixel at the star's glow). `window.dice = {ready(key, fn), snapshot(key, fn), boot(), show(id), world}` is the page's bridge (in the Tetra template): both worlds register their start and snapshot functions with it, and one hot-reload hook starts both from one snapshot (each used to register its own, and only one survived, so Tetra came up blank after a republish). The hook adds `setDay`, `setYaw`, `date()`, `moon()`, `phase(id)`, `follow`, `release`, `starScreen()`, `sunDir()`, `moonDie()` ({sides, count, tip, flat} of the die mesh: 5 sides, 60 vertices, the tip beyond the flats; `../tetra/tests/cuba.js` checks it), and `volcano()` ({erupting, screen, sparks, facing}: the test checks it is quiet on an ordinary day, erupting with sparks on the Restart Day, and north of the Canyon on screen). `neighbours()` reports which side is left, right, up, down and behind the facing side on screen. Hot reload (`window.claude.hot`) restores spin, camera and play state, as Tetra does.
