# Octa, the eight-sided planet (The Dice System)

Octa is the third world of Ruby's Dice System, the d8, added on 2026-10-17 (Ruby: "cool now lets add octa (or octo ...)"). It ships inside Tetra's page, like Hexa: `pipeline/world.html` is a fragment (scoped CSS under `#oapp`, ids starting with `o`, the hook `window.octa`) that `../tetra/pipeline/build.py` inserts at `{{OCTA}}`. It has no map pipeline yet: nothing on it is discovered or drawn (Ruby: "so the first side hasent been discoverd or drawn yet"), so the fragment draws its eight blank sides itself, on a canvas, each with its number on the die. Tetra's rules apply (read `../tetra/CLAUDE.md`): never invent canon, no em dashes anywhere, test before shipping, Ruby's spellings exactly inside quotes. Read `HANDOFF.md` for the ledger and the open questions.

## What exists (draft 5, 2026-10-18)

- The names: Ruby's rule of 2026-10-17 for every planet, "witchever is the hedron name minus \"hedron\"": Octa, Icosa (Icoso before), Icositetra (Icosotetra before). Before the rule, Octa by the vote Ruby called for ("me, daddy, and you will vote (daddy votes octa, i vote octo)"): Ruby Octo, Daddy Octa, Claude Octa (the pattern of her Cuba renaming, since made her rule, and the family sound of Tetra, Hexa, Octa, Deca, Dodeca). Two to one. She can overrule it.
- The sides: eight, named by their number on the die for now (Ruby: "the sides are just named by the number on the dice"), until she picks two more side names (her word of 2026-10-18, later again; "directions" in her first message) to join Hexa's six or keeps the numbers. The World tab lists six pairs fixed to the body of the die (Zenith and Nadir, Peak and Deep, Over and Under, Crown and Keel, High and Low, Upper and Lower), the first five of 2026-10-17 as asked ("give me at least 3 examples"), then five more ("give me another 5 so they arent confusing"), since her fourth message more with nothing of up and down (`#odirections2`: Hither and Yon, Bow and Stern, Port and Starboard; Spinward and Counterspin and Lesser and Greater were withdrawn by her fifth message), and since her fifth message five names for two opposite sides (`#odirections3`: Heads and Tails, Obverse and Reverse, Home and Away, Recto and Verso, Warp and Weft); the pairs tied to the star or the view (Dawn and Dusk, Windward and Leeward, Sunward and Starward, and the like) were withdrawn on 2026-10-18 after "you do not realise that it rotates so that nay face could be seen". None is chosen.
- The die: a regular octahedron (`R` = 1.45, corners on the axes). `OCTANTS` gives each number its octant: 1, 3, 5, 7 round the top corner (1 facing the viewer at the start, then round to the viewer's left), 2, 4, 6, 8 their opposites, so opposite sides add up to 9 as on a d8. Which number sits where is a reading. Each side's picture (`sidePicture`) is slate with a dashed edge, its number and "not yet discovered"; `TEX_SCALE` maps the side's own plane onto a 512 px canvas.
- Since her second message of the day ("give octa the same star, a d7 moon, a turn, a calender with one more month than hexa and a restart day that is one and three fourths of a day pleese"): Tetra's star (`SUN0`, the glow in `drawStars`, the sides lit by it, dimmed by an eclipse); the die turns once a day leftward (`spinAngle`, `cam.model`, `worldNormal`; the camera can follow a side as on Hexa); the d7 moon (`moonMesh`: a five-sided prism, `MOON_PRISM` = 0.75 by the solid-angle rule, faces 0 to 4 oblong with one to five craters, 5 and 6 the pentagon ends with six and seven, `FS2`; `moonState` and `moonModel` as Hexa's: opposite the star, the swing on the 14th, rolling once a day); the calendar (`WORLD.calendar`: 15 months of 28 days, the fifteenth unnamed (after Amberary in draft 2, between March and April since her third message), the Restart Day 1.75 days after December, `YEAR` 421.75, `dateParts`, Tetra's day names); phases on the side cards (`phaseOf`, `sideLight`). Readings everywhere Ruby gave only the headline: the moon's shape and ways, the turn's direction and the day's length, the week's names. Unwritten and not drawn after that message (filled in by the next): size, fields, seasons, the year count, Octa's place among the worlds.
- Since her third message ("EVERYTHING IS SIMILAR TO THE FIRST TWO", "the 15th month goes between march and april, but is still unnamed", "you shuld be able to see all of the other planets from a given planet", "a ship as landed on side one of octa"): the two fields as rings (`ringPoints`, `drawFields`, `occluded` with `octaTris`), rain in the rain season (`rainAmount`, `drawRain` on the sides' own planes), Hexa's seasons (`seasonName`, the fifteenth month's season unwritten until her fourth message), the months with the fifteenth between March and April, years from the ship's landing (`yearNow` 1 then, 0 since her fourth message), the sky block shared with Tetra and Hexa (`SKY_ALL`, `SKY`, `drawSkyBody`, `drawOtherWorld`, `skyBodies()`; this world is `SKY_HOME` octa), and one marker, The ship's landing on side 1 (`WORLD.places.landing`, `buildMarkers`, `updateMarkers`, `selectPlace`). The direction pairs are the body-fixed ones.
- Since her fourth message ("the first half of the 15th month is spring, the secont half is in betwee nspring and summer", "it shuld be year 0 of octa", "Why are all the planets not in a curved line from te sun", "why is octa's magical field the same as hexa, it shuld be stronger", "can i pleese have 5 more pairs that also dont have anything to do with up and down?"): the fifteenth month's season split at its middle (`seasonName`, the `UNNAMED` branch: spring, then between spring and summer), `yearNow` 0 with -0 before it (`yl` in `dateParts`, Tetra's rule that -0 and 0 are different years, a reading), the shared sky block laying every planet on one arc from the star (since her fifth message the arc runs through this world: `layoutSky` lays it out on the screen each frame, `SKY_R`, the planets nearer the star between the star and the die, the farther ones beyond it; the order along it still the reading of 2026-10-04), four gold rings thicker and brighter than Hexa's (`RINGS.gold`, `drawFields`; the blue as Hexa's), and a second list of pairs with nothing of up and down (`#odirections2`: Hither and Yon, Bow and Stern, Port and Starboard; Lesser and Greater and Spinward and Counterspin were in it until her fifth message; Odd and Even noted as one that would repeat High and Low in this layout).
- Since her fifth message ("\"Why are all the planets not in a curved line from te sun\" was including the planet you are on. theese are side names, not directions, and we arent judgeing the greateness of one side to be better than another, pleese coan i have 3 to 5 more pairs? maps are being drawn of (what is curently side one) and will be sent to tetra to update the model system soon"): the sky line runs through the world you stand on (`layoutSky`, shared by the three fragments), the direction pairs are offered as side names (Spinward and Counterspin and Lesser and Greater withdrawn, `#odirections3` added), and the Drawn so far row says her maps of side 1 are coming.
- The dock shows the date ("Starday · 1 January"; "Restart Day · 1¾ days"; "Starday · day 3 of the unnamed month"), "Year N of Octa", the season and the day of the year of 421¾, the Spin and Day sliders, Play/Pause, −1 and +1 year, Reset, the eight side buttons and the Codex button; the Spin slider and Play/Pause act on the shared clock (`dice.time`, through getters on `state` as in Hexa).
- Codex: World (the world, the vote, the side names question, the Dice System list, Still unwritten), Sides (the eight, each "Not discovered or drawn yet", side 1 in Ruby's words), Side (the chosen side's card with its opposite, and on side 1 the landing's place card).
- Hook `window.octa`: `state`, `facing()`, `sides()`, `opposite(id)`, `face(id, fly)`, `setYaw(yaw, pitch)`, `setSpin(on)`, `syncTime()`, `setDay(d)`, `date()`, `months()`, `yearDays()`, `moonDie()`, `moon()`, `phase(id)`, `starScreen()`, `sunDir()`, `release()`, `skyBodies()`, `otherDir()`, `markers()`, `selectPlace(id)`, `home()` (the camera back to its home view), `centre(id)` (screen point of a side's centre), `sample(id)` and `sampleAt(fx, fy)` (pixels). `../tetra/tests/octa.js` uses them.
- Bridge: `dice.ready('octa', start)` and `dice.snapshot('octa', ...)` (baseYaw, pitch, dist, selectedSide); `dice.show('octa')` toggles `away` on `#oapp`; `dice.world` reads `octa` when it is shown.
