The world

CUBAThe cube planet · The Dice System

A world with six square sides, the second world of Ruby’s Dice System after Tetra.

Sides
Six, named like Tetra’s with two more: Canon, East, West, Far, North and South. North is the top and South the bottom. Looking at Canon, East is on its left, West on its right and Far behind it.
Drawn so far
The Canon side: the Plus Continent, whose four arms reach the sides and connect onto the East, West, North and South sides, with the Canyon in its middle, the small hole that takes you through the world to the Far side. The other five sides are not yet charted beyond that.
Size
About 20,000 miles from one end of the Canyon to the other, probably: as long as Tetra is from the corner of Spikia through to Mount Celestial (Ruby). The Canyon runs straight through, so that is the length of every edge (a reading).
Turn
The same way as Tetra, leftward, and a day the same length as Tetra’s: one full turn, 25 hours.
The star
Tetra’s dark purple star, seen from farther out. It stays where it is: turn the world to find it, as Ruby asked.
Tetra
Visible in Cuba’s sky as a small four-sided world toward the star (Ruby: “you shuld be able to see tetra from cuba and vice versa”); where exactly it stands is a reading.
Fields
One magical and one magnetic, both a little stronger than Tetra’s (Ruby): the gold and blue rings around the world, hidden only where the world itself is in front of them.
Place
Farther from the star than Tetra. Ruby: “if there were 5 magical fields, and tetra was in the first one, cuba would be in the last one (going away from the star would be the direction they were stacked in)”.

The calendar

Months
14 · 28 days each: Tetra’s thirteen and Amberary, the fourteenth, placed between January and February (Ruby). Counted Ruby’s way, from March, the strip below runs that way, with January, Amberary, February and Tredesember at its end, after the Restart Day.
Year
392 days + the Restart Day, which on Cuba is one and a half days long: 393½ days, “same as tetra but probably one month longer”. The year begins on 1 January, after the Restart Day, as on Tetra.
Years
Counted from Amber’s landing: “when amber reached cuba, she clled it year one of cuba (so rigt now it is year 50)”. It is year 50 of Cuba while it is 1700 on Tetra.
Week
Seven days with Tetra’s names: Starday, Moonday, Goldday, Blueday, Spikeday, Wetday, Twilightday (Ruby: “same names for days on cuba also”). 392 month days are 56 weeks, so with the Restart Day outside the week every month runs from a Starday to a Twilightday (a reading, as on Tetra).
One clock
Tetra and Cuba share their days: a day here is a day there, and pausing, spinning or stepping time on one does it on both. Their years and months are their own, so when Tetra steps from Tredesember into March, Cuba is just starting Tredesember.
Amberary
“half in rain seoson half in transition between rain and spring” (Ruby): the first fourteen days are rain season, the last fourteen the time between rain and spring.
Seasons
The rain season runs through the Restart Day, January and the first half of Amberary; then the time between rain and spring; then spring from February. The months from February on keep Tetra’s seasons until Ruby says otherwise: a reading. In the rain season it rains on every side (Ruby), though never on the moon or the star.

    The moon

    “the moon of tetra is similar to cuba” and “a d5 probably” (Ruby). So Cuba’s moon is Tetra’s in every way but the die: a five-sided die, a third of Cuba’s size, circling a little farther out than the magical field, opposite the star on ordinary days, swinging round in front of the star on the 14th of every month for a solar eclipse, rolling once a day, waxing and waning through the month. Ruby first allowed “the round shape used for equal oucomes in the real world, or is just a square piramid”, then wrote “te piramid does not grant equal chances so whatever grants equal chances for bouth moons is good” and “a fair d3 for tetra and a fair d5 for cuba”. So the d5 is a fair d5: a five-sided long die, a pentagonal rod with one to five craters in a row along each of its five long sides, and pointed ends, so it can only ever come to rest on a side. That it is a rod rather than another fair shape is a reading.

    Life of Cuba

    Ruby: “add most of the recognizable plants from the real world to bouth and add more umbrela tree zinias on cuba than on tetra (cince there is more majic)”, and “anything that grows on tetra also grows on cuba”. So everything that grows on Tetra grows here, most of the plants you would know from Earth among them, for example . The Umbrella Tree Zinnia, whose stem is not wood and whose top is petals, grows here more than on Tetra, since there is more magic. Which plants grow where is unwritten.

    The Montezuma. Ruby: “cuba has a gian tree called montezuma that grow ut to a 15th of the hight of mount celectial”. A giant tree of Cuba, growing up to a fifteenth of the height of Tetra’s Mount Celestial. Worked out from the build, not her word: Mount Celestial there stands 0.6 of the moon’s length (her “at least half”), so a full-grown Montezuma would be about 330 miles tall if Tetra’s edge is about 24,500 miles (about 270 miles if the mountain were exactly half the moon’s length). Where it grows, whether it is one tree or a kind of tree, and whether it also grows on Tetra, is unwritten.

    The Plus Continent and the Canyon

    From Ruby’s picture of the Canon side (2026-10-04): a broad island shaped like a cross, with an arm toward the middle of each side and rounded bends at the corners. In the middle she wrote “to far side”: the small hole that takes you to the opposite side of the world. Ruby: “the arms connect to the north south east and west faces”, so the land crosses those four edges; how far it goes on each of those sides is unwritten, and it is drawn stopping a short way in. The island’s shape is drawn from the description of her picture. Ruby named them: “the hole is called the canyon, the island is called ‘the plus continent’”. And “you can come thugh the hole ither way, so the far side will probably get explored next”.

    How Cuba was reached

    “lets imagine that amber the explorer makes a chamber out of a tree and (calculated corectly) launches of ither spikia or the far side using majic, gets flung by the moving moon (going in or out of solar eclipse) gets flung by the star and lands on the cannon face of cuba (year 1650)”

    Ruby, 2026-10-04, in her words. Amber the explorer, in a chamber made from a tree, launched by magic from Tetra, flung on by the moving moon at an eclipse and then by the star, and landed on the Canon side of Cuba in the year 1650. She launched from Tetra’s Far side (Ruby: “8: far side”).

    The explorers

    Ruby: “5 people also came with amber, they are all explorers: Ammolite, Aquamarine, Axinite, Benitoite, and Chrysoberyl (they are for exploring the other sides when we get there)”. Then: “ammolite has desided to go down the canyon”, and “Ammolite has drawn a map and is heading back down to the cannon side”. Her map has not reached the page yet.
