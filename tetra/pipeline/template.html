    The two fields

    Magical field · slows lightGoes all the way around the world. It slows light, so dawn crosses the land slowly and the first light comes in gold, and it turns the dark purple star’s light into the colour of our own sunlight.Here, the morning moves slower. And light flows like gold.
    Magnetic field · guides worldsGoes all the way around the world too. It guides worlds.

    The star

    A dark purple starTetra’s sun is a dark purple star. Its light reaches the ground the colour of our own sun’s light, because the magical field turns it on the way in.

    The moon

    A d3 moonTetra’s moon is a three-sided die, a d3, and not the cube kind: one crater on its one side, two craters on its two side, three craters on its three side. It is a third of the size of Tetra, circles a little farther out than the magical field, and rolls slowly so every side comes round. Every ordinary day it sits on the opposite side from the star, drifting gently about that spot. Every month, in the middle of the month, is the new moon: the die swings round in front of the star and the world goes dark, a solar eclipse. Its light waxes and wanes through the month like our own moon’s, full when the month turns, and the Restart Day is never a new moon.
