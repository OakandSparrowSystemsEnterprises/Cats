The world

HEXAThe cube planet · The Dice System

A world with six square sides, the second world of Ruby’s Dice System after Tetra.

Sides
Six, named like Tetra’s with two more: Canon, East, West, Far, North and South. North is the top and South the bottom. Looking at Canon, East is on its left, West on its right and Far behind it.
Drawn so far
The Canon side: the Plus Continent, whose four arms reach the sides and connect onto the East, West, North and South sides, with the Canyon in its middle, the small hole that takes you through the world to the Far side. The Far side, from Ruby’s words of 2026-10-05, “the far side has a main island about a quarter of the sise of the face with land on the north east and south east corners, making it half water. on the central island, north of the canyon, there is a volcano (active) (shoots lava every reset day)”: the main island around the Canyon’s other end, land on the north-east and south-east corners, half water, and the volcano north of the Canyon, erupting every Restart Day. The North, East, South and West sides (2026-10-06): drawn from Ruby’s sketches of the explorers’ maps (Aquamarine, Axinite, Benitoite, Chrysoberyl), with the Plus Continent’s arms reaching in as far as the sketches show, and since 2026-10-10 the two portals in the sea on North and West (Ruby: “The rings that were drawn as “islands” were actualy portals. No crescent land, bouth spiral islands are portals in the ocean.”). Nothing on those four sides is named yet.
The name
Hexa, since Ruby’s line of 2026-10-10: “Hey the hedron name for a cube was a hexahedron So Cuba should be called hexa.” It was Cuba from 2026-10-04 until then.
Size
About 20,000 miles from one end of the Canyon to the other, probably: as long as Tetra is from the corner of Spikia through to Mount Celestial (Ruby). The Canyon runs straight through, so that is the length of every edge (a reading).
Turn
The same way as Tetra, leftward, and a day the same length as Tetra’s: one full turn, 25 hours.
The star
Tetra’s dark purple star, seen from farther out. It stays where it is: turn the world to find it, as Ruby asked.
Tetra
Visible in Hexa’s sky as a small four-sided world toward the star (Ruby: “you shuld be able to see tetra from cuba and vice versa”); where exactly it stands is a reading.
Fields
One magical and one magnetic, both a little stronger than Tetra’s (Ruby): the gold and blue rings around the world, hidden only where the world itself is in front of them.
Place
Farther from the star than Tetra. Ruby: “if there were 5 magical fields, and tetra was in the first one, cuba would be in the last one (going away from the star would be the direction they were stacked in)”.

The calendar

Months
14 · 28 days each: Tetra’s thirteen and Amberary, the fourteenth, placed between January and February (Ruby). Counted Ruby’s way, from March, the strip below runs that way, with January, Amberary, February and Tredesember at its end, after the Restart Day.
Year
392 days + the Restart Day, which on Hexa is one and a half days long: 393½ days, “same as tetra but probably one month longer”. The year begins on 1 January, after the Restart Day, as on Tetra.
Years
Counted from Amber’s landing: “when amber reached cuba, she clled it year one of cuba (so rigt now it is year 50)”. It is year 50 of Hexa while it is 1700 on Tetra.
Week
Seven days with Tetra’s names: Starday, Moonday, Goldday, Blueday, Spikeday, Wetday, Twilightday (Ruby: “same names for days on cuba also”). 392 month days are 56 weeks, so with the Restart Day outside the week every month runs from a Starday to a Twilightday (a reading, as on Tetra).
One clock
Tetra and Hexa share their days: a day here is a day there, and pausing, spinning or stepping time on one does it on both. Their years and months are their own, so when Tetra steps from Tredesember into March, Hexa is just starting Tredesember.
Amberary
“half in rain seoson half in transition between rain and spring” (Ruby): the first fourteen days are rain season, the last fourteen the time between rain and spring.
Seasons
The rain season runs through the Restart Day, January and the first half of Amberary; then the time between rain and spring; then spring from February. The months from February on have Tetra’s seasons (Ruby, 2026-10-06: “same as tetra”). In the rain season it rains on every side (Ruby), though never on the moon or the star.

    The moon

    “the moon of tetra is similar to cuba” and “a d5 probably” (Ruby). So Hexa’s moon is Tetra’s in every way but the die: a five-sided die, a third of Hexa’s size, circling a little farther out than the magical field, opposite the star on ordinary days, swinging round in front of the star on the 14th of every month for a solar eclipse, rolling once a day, waxing and waning through the month. Ruby first allowed “the round shape used for equal oucomes in the real world, or is just a square piramid”, then wrote “te piramid does not grant equal chances so whatever grants equal chances for bouth moons is good” and “a fair d3 for tetra and a fair d5 for cuba”. So the d5 is a fair d5: a five-sided long die, a pentagonal rod with one to five craters in a row along each of its five long sides, and pointed ends, so it can only ever come to rest on a side. That it is a rod rather than another fair shape is a reading.

    Life of Hexa

    Ruby: “add most of the recognizable plants from the real world to bouth and add more umbrela tree zinias on cuba than on tetra (cince there is more majic)”, and “anything that grows on tetra also grows on cuba”. So everything that grows on Tetra grows here, most of the plants you would know from Earth among them, for example . The Umbrella Tree Zinnia, whose stem is not wood and whose top is petals, grows here more than on Tetra, since there is more magic. Ruby, 2026-10-06, on which plants grow where: “i don't know yet (earth planets grow all over)”, so Earth’s plants grow all over Hexa, and the colours of the land are Tetra’s (“same as tetra”).

    The Montezuma. Ruby: “cuba has a gian tree called montezuma that grow ut to a 15th of the hight of mount celectial”. Then: “it grows to be 1 mile high and 1.5 mile radius in canopy and a 3/4 mile wide trunk it will grow on the east side of cuba when it is writen”. So: a giant tree of Hexa, a mile high, with a canopy a mile and a half in radius and a trunk three quarters of a mile wide, growing up to a fifteenth of the height of Tetra’s Mount Celestial; it will grow on the East side once that side is written, and nothing is drawn until then (the East side is drawn from her sketch since 2026-10-06; whether that is “writen” enough for the tree is hers to say). Her two lines together make Mount Celestial fifteen miles high, which does not fit the mountain in the build (0.6 of the moon’s length, far taller); which to keep is hers to say. Whether it is one tree or a kind of tree, and whether it also grows on Tetra, is unwritten.

    The Plus Continent and the Canyon

    From Ruby’s picture of the Canon side (2026-10-04): a broad island shaped like a cross, with an arm toward the middle of each side and rounded bends at the corners. In the middle she wrote “to far side”: the small hole that takes you to the opposite side of the world. Ruby: “the arms connect to the north south east and west faces”, so the land crosses those four edges; how far it goes on each of those sides she does not know yet (2026-10-06: “i don't know yet”); it is drawn as far as her sketches of the explorers’ maps show. The island’s shape is drawn from the description of her picture. Ruby named them: “the hole is called the canyon, the island is called ‘the plus continent’”. And “you can come thugh the hole ither way, so the far side will probably get explored next”.

    How Hexa was reached

    “lets imagine that amber the explorer makes a chamber out of a tree and (calculated corectly) launches of ither spikia or the far side using majic, gets flung by the moving moon (going in or out of solar eclipse) gets flung by the star and lands on the cannon face of cuba (year 1650)”

    Ruby, 2026-10-04, in her words. Amber the explorer, in a chamber made from a tree, launched by magic from Tetra, flung on by the moving moon at an eclipse and then by the star, and landed on the Canon side of Hexa in the year 1650. She launched from Tetra’s Far side (Ruby: “8: far side”).

    The Dice System

    Ruby, 2026-10-10: “We have for planets: a d4 a d6 a d8 a d10 a d12 a d20 a d24 a d30. So 8 planets. Maby make some d0.5’s for asteroids And make a belt? So to recap, tetra, hexa, octo, deca, dodeca, icoso, icosotetra, and triaconta are our planets.” and “Also probably shuld add the d2 and d1 as dwarf planets”.

      Where the other six sit, in which fields and how far from the star, is unwritten; the d0.5 asteroids and a belt are her question, not built.

Then: “ammolite has desided to go down the canyon”, and “Ammolite has drawn a map and is heading back down to the cannon side”. Her map has not reached the page yet. On 2026-10-06: “North side explored by aquamarine”, “East side discovered by axinite the explorer”, “South side discover by benitoite the explorer”, “Chrysoberyl is waiting until she’s done resting”, “Chrysoberyl’s map of west side”, with her sketches of those four sides, which the sides are now drawn from.
